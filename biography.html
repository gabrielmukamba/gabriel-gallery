My Story

Gabriel Mukamba is a professional English communicator, aspiring programmer, and automobile engineering enthusiast from the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC). He was born on 2 April in Baraka, South Kivu, where he spent his early years developing a strong passion for learning, technology, and personal growth.

From an early age, Gabriel demonstrated curiosity, determination, and a desire to acquire practical skills. In 2015, he began studying automobile engineering, building a solid foundation in vehicle maintenance, diagnostics, and mechanical repair. His dedication and commitment enabled him to successfully earn his high school diploma in 2023.

In 2024, Gabriel relocated to Kampala, Uganda, to pursue advanced English communication and expand his professional opportunities. Since then, he has devoted himself to mastering English, believing that effective communication is one of the most powerful tools for leadership, education, and international collaboration.

Beyond communication, Gabriel has practical experience in automobile mechanics, professional driving, computer literacy, and programming. He possesses a strong understanding of computer fundamentals and continues to develop his programming skills in languages such as Python and C++, with the goal of creating innovative software solutions and contributing to the technology industry. He has already built and shipped several working projects, including a full-stack AI-powered engineering translation tool, a civil engineering concrete calculator, and multiple web applications — all documented in his project portfolio.

Gabriel is passionate about continuous learning, self-improvement, and helping others through knowledge sharing. He believes that discipline, integrity, perseverance, and lifelong learning are the foundations of lasting success. His vision is to become an internationally recognized English communication professional, software developer, educator, and entrepreneur who inspires young people to pursue excellence regardless of their background.

Today, Gabriel continues to strengthen his expertise in English communication, technology, and professional development while working toward building a career that positively impacts individuals and communities both in Africa and around the world.
